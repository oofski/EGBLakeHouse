Document 2 — Lake House Manual

LAKE HOUSE MANUAL

Edgeless Beauty Group  •  N53W34945 Road B, Okauchee, WI 53069

Welcome

Welcome to the Edgeless Beauty Group Lake House on beautiful Okauchee Lake. We hope you enjoy a relaxing and rejuvenating stay. Please read this manual carefully — it contains everything you need for a safe and comfortable visit.

Check-In & Check-Out

  • Check-In: 4:00 PM. A keyless entry code will be sent to you 24 hours before your arrival.
  • Check-Out: 10:00 AM. Please complete the departure checklist before you leave.

House Rules

  • Maximum occupancy is 8 people.
  • No smoking anywhere inside the residence.
  • No parties or events of any kind.
  • Quiet hours are 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM.
  • Maximum of 3 cars on the property.
  • Please remove shoes inside and treat the home with care.

Okauchee Lake Rules

  • Observe all posted no-wake zones, especially within 100 feet of shore, piers, and swimmers.
  • Slow-no-wake hours apply from sunset to 10:00 AM.
  • Be courteous to neighboring properties and other lake users.
  • Do not disturb wildlife or shoreline vegetation.

Boat Use Requirements

  • Operators must have 50+ logged hours of boating experience.
  • A valid boating license is required and must be approved by the admin in advance.
  • Operating under the influence (OUI) is strictly prohibited.
  • Return the boat to the lift and connect the charger after every use.

Life Jacket Requirements

  • Wisconsin law requires a properly fitted, U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jacket for every person on board.
  • Children under 16 must wear a life jacket at all times on or near the water — please bring one per minor.

Emergency Contacts

  • Emergency: 911
  • Non-Emergency (Sheriff): 262-446-5070
  • Property Manager — Susan Haise: 414-397-2882
  • Wisconsin DNR: 1-800-943-0003

Local Restaurants

  • The Golden Mast Inn — Lakeside German & American, Okauchee Lake
  • Weissgerber's Seven Seas — Seafood & steaks on Nagawicka Lake, Hartland
  • Knuckleheads Pub & Grill — Casual lakeside fare, Okauchee
  • Stonewood Ale House — Craft beer & pub food, Oconomowoc
  • The Beach House — Waterfront American dining, Oconomowoc Lake
  • Ginza of Tokyo — Japanese & sushi, Delafield
  • Ferrante's Signature Cuisine — Italian, Delafield

Fishing Guide

  • Okauchee Lake is home to musky, walleye, largemouth bass, northern pike, and panfish.
  • A valid Wisconsin fishing license is required for anyone 16 and older (available at gowild.wi.gov).
  • Observe all DNR size and bag limits. Please practice catch-and-release for musky.
  • Rinse and stow any borrowed fishing gear after use.

Departure Checklist

  • Start the dishwasher and dispose of all trash in the bins outside.
  • Turn off all lights and set the thermostat to 65°F (winter) / 75°F (summer).
  • Close and lock all windows and doors.
  • Return the boat and any watercraft to their lifts and connect chargers.
  • Take all personal belongings — check closets and drawers.
  • Report any damage to Susan Haise at 414-397-2882.
